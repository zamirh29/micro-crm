MicroCRM is a straightforward CRM and accounting app for UK sole traders, freelancers and small businesses. Create customers, send invoices and quotes, track expenses, and see exactly where your money is going — without spreadsheet chaos.

BUILT FOR UK SOLE TRADERS
• Invoices and quotes with your own logo, payment terms and VAT settings
• Record payments and see what is still outstanding at a glance
• Track expenses and attach receipts
• Dashboard with income, expenses and profit for the current period

TAX AND HMRC
• Tax report with profit estimates and tax-year bands
• Month-end report for your bookkeeping
• HMRC Making Tax Digital support: connect your HMRC account and view open obligations
• Accounting exports (CSV/PDF) you can hand to your accountant

WORK FROM ANYWHERE
• Import customers and invoices from a spreadsheet or PDF

• Everything syncs with your MicroCRM account, so the web dashboard and the app stay in step

STAY ON TOP OF THINGS

• Activity log with restore, so a wrong edit is never permanent
• Scheduled report emails (Pro)
• Multiple businesses and team access

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
• Your data stays in your own MicroCRM workspace
• Sign in with your existing MicroCRM account — no separate sign-up

Pro features such as tax reports, month-end reports and automated report emails are optional and can be unlocked inside the app.

## Data safety summary (to confirm in the form)

Collected:
- Personal info: name, email address, phone number (account and customer records)
- Financial info: invoices, payments, expenses (user-entered)
- Photos: receipt/import images (processed on device, only extracted text is uploaded)
- App activity: in-app actions logged to the activity feed

- Encrypted in transit: yes
- Data deletion request: available in app/account settings and by email
- Data shared with third parties: no (HMRC only when the user connects their own HMRC account)
